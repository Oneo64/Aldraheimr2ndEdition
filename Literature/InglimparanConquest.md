# The Inglimparan Conquest
The year is about 1800 BCE. The Kingdom of Inglimpara launched a conquest, now retold as the Inglimparan Conquest. They sought to conquer the world, assailing the Kingdoms of Hathklosh, Borusdalis, and Kiliidhuuk.

After many years, the powerful Kingdom of Haear collapsed in 1760 BCE. The kingdom was split into Borusdalis and Veilodin, but Inglimpara was quick to destroy Borusdalis. The people of Borusdalis fled east and formed Borusdaliandur.

After many years, the kingdoms of Borusdaliandur and Veilodin both banded together and worked to defeat Inglimpara. They targeted Inglimpara-occupied Kiliidhuuk and captured its capital.

After a long time of planning and preparation, the two kingdoms were ready for the final Invasion of Inglimpara. They attacked the capital at night, successfully taking over Inglimpara with the help of the darkness. During the battle, the conqueror was slain by an arrow fired by a knight of the Borusdaliandurian king, ending the conquest once and for all.
