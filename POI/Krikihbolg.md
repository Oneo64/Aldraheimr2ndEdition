# Krikihbolg

According to the tale, Krikihbolg is a massive fortress built inside an enormous cavern. Most of it has already crumbled and has been lying under dust for decades. Somewhere inside the fortress, there exists a room with a dimensional gateway that leads to Sotjørð. Although the gateway frame itself still stands, a chunk of it has been blown off.

Unfortunately, the fortress is only known through the tale, and there isn't really anyone alive who has seen it. No one is sure who built the fortress or how they built it.
