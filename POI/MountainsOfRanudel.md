The Mountains of Ranûdel are a very mountainous region in the southeast lands of Myrkjorðr. Instead of typical mountains, they are karsts and jagged rocky peaks. Surrounding the region is an ethereal blue mist that has never dissipated once, called the Cûlentai by the natives. This misty atmosphere has a chilly feeling and blocks out the sun from ever reaching the forest.

The forest that lives on these mountains consists of large, crooked trees like oaks and elms, and small, bent trees like fairy trees. On the forest floor, there is heather and a variety of ghostly-looking flowers and magical-looking berries.
