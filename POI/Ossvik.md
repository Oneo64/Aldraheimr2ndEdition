Óssvík is a coastal village east of Oddarsveldi. It has houses built neatly together, boat-keeping houses near the sandy shore, and a bridge across a river mouth to the north. Surrounding the village is an open meadow, with dandelions and small white flowers scattered across the field, blooming in Spring and Summer.

The river mouth is shallow, yet still dangerous to cross. It's better to go over the bridge.

## Trading
Travelling merchants who live here typically have a small karve, taking it up the Álahringr-Óssvík route to trade fish for wood. Some take the route south to Saequor (Kingdom of Centruma) to trade iron, fish, wool, and other exports.
