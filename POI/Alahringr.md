Álahringr is one of the southernmost villages in Oddarsveldi. It was the battlefield of the last invasion attempt by Vatakond, as part of the Campaign of Wulfscithia.

Álahringr is a fairly normal-sized village. Because it is connected to the main road and is one of the closest villages to Rotherland and Centruma, travellers occasionally pass through. The hersir's house and the community hall are both located north of the village (closer to friendly territory).

Around Álahringr is a mix of terrain: to the west is an open grass field, to the east is the Southern Northmannish Forest, and to the west is a river that leads to a small dinghy port.

There is a small path that leads into the forest, eventually stopping at an abandoned longhouse. This house is run-down and is deteriorating, its wood rotten and its frame crumbling.

## Trading
Some merchants take a boat along the southern river to trade wood and berries for fish with Óssvík.

## Local beliefs
- **Troll of the old longhouse:** It is believed that the ghost of a troll dwells in that old longhouse, and anyone who is caught by the ghost might get cursed by it.
- **Centaur bias:** A lot of people have a bias against centaurs due to a raid last year from the neighbouring centaur kingdom Vatakond.
