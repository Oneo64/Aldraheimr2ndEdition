Saequor is a seaside town in Centruma. It is a major trading spot with Oddarsveldi. Around town is a tall wooden wall and a gate, a crucial defensive structure against the unknown.

Houses in Saequor are small with limestone bricks and tiled roofs. Most of them have just one floor with doors made of softwood. The community center is built in the middle of the town and consists of two floors. The ground is paved with pebbles, and there are wooden torch poles for when night falls. At the coast is a harbour with three ports.

The main inn here is owned by an old man named Oddius, who provides rooms with a single bed, table, and candle.

Surrounding the town is a small grass field, and the road leading out of town goes straight into the Centrumic Forest.

## Trade
Saeqor is a major point in the Saequor-Óssvík trade route, where merchants trade iron, fish, wool, and other things.
