# Úlfsvening

Úlfsvening is a natural point of interest near the eastern end of the Nordic Mountain Range. At the tip of one of the forested mountains, there is a stony peak with an overhang. One can see over the entire forest that covers this mountain, making it an excellent vacation spot (except for the wolves, bears, and other dangerous stuff).
