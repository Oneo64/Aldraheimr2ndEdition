The Ruins of Yngvarr's End, known as Yngvarendavirki by the northmen, is a small stone fortress built inside the forest on the Lantheressil Peninsula. The castle now lies in ruins after suffering a devastating siege, overgrown with vines and weeds.

A few years ago, a group of adventurers tried to explore the ruins, but instead encountered a gvarth and a draugr, and narrowly escaped with several wounds.
