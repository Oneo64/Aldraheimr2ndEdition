# Moon Shrines
<table><tbody>
	<tr> <th colspan=2>Moon Shrines</th> </tr>
	<tr> <td>Region</td> <td>North and central Myrkjörðr</td> </tr>

Moon shrines are small, limestone altars built around the world. Each altar has a small bell on top. There is a popular rumour that taking the bell from a shrine will curse the thief. A popular rumour amongst elven societies is that they were built by elven spirits.
