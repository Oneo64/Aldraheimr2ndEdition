Centruma is a major kingdom located in central Myrkjörðr. The capital city is Lantum.

## Miltiary
The Centrumian army has about 2500 men, 1200 in Lantum and 700 in Vero. Soldiers live in garrisons.

### Uniforms
- **Spearman:** Spearmen's uniforms consist of a leather skullcap, leather breastplate, white tunic, grey pants, and shoes. Their main loadout is a spear and a red kite shield.
- **Bowman:** Bowmen's uniforms consist of a leather skullcap, leather breastplate, white tunic, grey pants, and shoes. Their main loadout is a longbow and a quiver of flinthead arrows.
- **Centurion:** Centurions' uniforms consist of a steel galea helmet, segmented armour (lorica segmentata), a white tunic, grey pants, and shoes. Their main loadout is a spear, a spatha, and a red round shield.
- **Palace guard:** Palace guards' uniforms consist of a steel galea helmet, a hauberk, a yellow surcoat, a white tunic, grey pants, and shoes. Their main loadout is a spear and a red kite shield.
