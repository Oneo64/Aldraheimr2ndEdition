# The Centrumic Forests

	<tr> <td>Region</td> <td>Centruma</td> </tr>

The Centrumic Forests are a collection of many smaller forests, all located to the south of Centruma. They are bright redwood forests with little undergrowth, mostly covered in podzol. These lands are rugged, unlike the flatter open fields near Centruma's civilisation.
