The Lantheressil Peninsula is covered by the Lantheressil Forest, a dark, murky forest of towering pines and overgrown ferns. An ethereal blue mist sits here, seemingly never dissipating from the peninsula, no matter how strong the wind is or how clear the sky is. There have been rumours of frost dragons living here.
