Barastaðr is a small settlement built around a low hill, with its houses scattered around. The hersir's house and the community hall are built on the top of the hill. There is a thin path to the southwest that leads to a well, which is covered with a wooden roof.

The landscape around Barataðr is rather empty; a desolate land with little grass and trees. The only bit of forest is about five kilometres away, a small wooded area of pines.

## Trade
Rye is mainly grown here because it is resistant to the cold and can grow in poor soil. Traders trade rye with other settlements for things like iron and other resources.
