The Heirfgrian Forest is a dark, murky redwood forest south of the Mountains of Ranûdel. The trees here are very tall, casting a gloomy shadow over the land, making sunlight scarce. Smaller trees grow poorly under the dominating redwoods, while prospering in rare glades. The centaurs of Heirfgria roam this forest, which has a moon shrine built somewhere within it.
