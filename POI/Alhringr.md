Álhringr is one of the southernmost villages in Oddarsveldi. Built along the main road, it is a major site for travellers and merchants. The village is fairly normal-sized. It was the battlefield of the last invasion attempt by Vatakond, as part of the Campaign of Wulfscithia.

Around Álhringr is a mix of several different types of landscapes: to the west is an open grass field, to the east is the Southern Northmannish Forest, and to the west is a river that leads to a small dinghy port.

There is a small path that leads into the forest, eventually stopping at an abandoned longhouse. This house is run-down and is deteriorating, its wood rotten and its frame crumbling.

## Trading
There is a trade route along the southern river, where merchants trade wood and berries for fish with Óssvík, a village on the coastal end of that river.

## Local beliefs
- **Troll of the old longhouse:** It is believed that the ghost of a troll dwells in that old longhouse, and anyone who is caught by the ghost might get cursed by it.
- **Centaur bias:** A lot of people have a bias against centaurs due to a raid last year from the neighbouring centaur kingdom Vatakond.
