Hersgjöf Fortress is a wooden ring fortress inside the Southern Northmannish Forest. Outside the walls are two acres of farmland, surrounded by a twig fence to keep out animals. It is the home of Jarl Hákon and his family.

Distress signals inside the fortress are two bonfire huts, which are small houses designed to hold a bonfire without interference from the rain or wind, and still allow smoke to leave by an opening in the ceiling. When one bonfire is lit, that means all is well. When two bonfires are lit, that means there is a threat.
