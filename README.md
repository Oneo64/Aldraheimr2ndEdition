# Aldraheimr (2nd Edition)
Because the files for the original world were messy and inconsistent, I present to you: **Aldraheimr (2nd Edition)!**

Aldraheimr is a mythic historical fantasy based on the 9th and 10th centuries. I will be trying a more narrative strategy in describing things and will also be trying to use more reference images, which should make things easier and more dynamic.

Everything in this world is either based on folklore, mythology, medieval texts, or my own imagination, and goes for that specific aesthetic that is mysterious and terrifying without grandeur. Although magic is rare and just strong enough to rival warriors without overpowering them, many still view magic as something that should be respected, feared, or hated.

The year will be set in NCE 235, and the abbreviation stands for New Centrumian Era. 1 NCE is the time when the Centrumian Realm was founded, so it's basically Anno Domini but different. Since I'm lazy, I will just say that BCE actually stands for Before Centrumian Era.

## Traveller's information
Take note of this, should you become a traveller.

You would have a spear or some farming tool in most cases. Unless your weapon requires two hands, you would probably have a wooden shield. You probably won't have any armour, unless it's a leather helmet or something, and even those are still uncommon.

Although it's generally safe to wander around a settlement, you should be cautious about travelling along the road or going into the wilderness. Despite magical encounters being relatively rare, you can still be ambushed by bandits if you aren't careful.

Taverns and inns are not commonplace anywhere in the world. You will probably find one in a city or a town, but they are not easy to get into (geographical problems or security reasons), and you will certainly not find one in some random village. If you really need lodging in a village, you'll have to ask the locals to give you a place to stay.

Magic can be scary because it's something you can't really understand, even if you were a Taribian sorcerer. Most practitioners of magic aren't any more effective in combat than warriors. However, nature's magic is still more powerful and weird than anything you could wield.

Monsters are eerie, unnerving, frightening, and unpredictable. Even if you practise magic or have armour, you won't be having an easy time if you encounter something hostile in the wilderness.
