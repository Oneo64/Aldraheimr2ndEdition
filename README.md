# Aldraheimr (2nd Edition)
Because the files for the original world were messy and inconsistent, I present to you: **Aldraheimr (2nd Edition)!**

Aldraheimr is a mythic historical fantasy based on the 9th and 10th centuries. In this edition, I will try a more narrative strategy in describing things, which should make things easier and more dynamic.

Everything in this world is either based on folklore or invented using a specific, mysterious, and terrifying aesthetic without being grandiose. Although magic exists here, it is rare and is just strong enough to rival warriors, but not overpower them. Many people view magic as something that should be respected, feared, or hated.

The year will be set in NCE 235, and the abbreviation stands for New Centrumian Era. 1 NCE is the time when the Centrumian Realm was founded, so it's basically Anno Domini but different. Since I'm lazy, I will just say that BCE actually stands for Before Centrumian Era.

## Traveller's information
Take note of this, should you become a traveller. Although it's generally safe to wander around a settlement, you should be cautious about travelling along the road or going into the wilderness. Despite magical encounters being relatively rare, you can still be ambushed by bandits if you aren't careful.
