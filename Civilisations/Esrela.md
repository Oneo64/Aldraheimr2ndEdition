The Kingdom of Esrela is a major kingdom located in the northern region of Myrkjörðr. Hathössali is the main palace of the kingdom.

## Military
The Esrel army has around 700 warriors, but most of them live in their homes.

### Uniforms
Esrel soldiers are required to bring their own weapons and armour. They must also wear a wood bead necklace, which is their signature.

Hathössali guards' uniforms consist of an iron nasal helmet, a hauberk, a blue cloak, and a wood bead necklace. Their main loadout is a flanged mace, longbow, and flinthead arrows.
