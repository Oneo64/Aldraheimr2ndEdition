The Kingdom of Ranûdel is a major kingdom located in the central region of Myrkjörðr, in the Mountains of Ranûdel.

## Military
The Ranûdan army has around 200 warriors in Endarassil. This doesn't count the local militia of other villages.

### Uniforms
- **Spearman:** Spearmen's uniforms consist of a green quilted gambeson, brown tunic, brown pants, and shoes. Their main loadout is a spear and a green kite shield.
- **Bowman:** Bowmen's uniforms consist of a green quilted gambeson, brown tunic, brown pants, and shoes. Their main loadout is a longbow and a quiver of obsidianhead arrows.
- **Palace guard:** Palace guards' uniforms consist of a green quilted gambeson, blue surcoat, brown tunic, brown pants, and shoes. Their main loadout is a glaive, a longbow, and a quiver of obsidianhead arrows.
