# Fungi
## Dark Lichen
Dark lichen can be found in Sotjörðr. It has a black appearance and has a greenish glow in the dark. It is poisonous, causing temporary blindness and nausea when eaten.

## Earthfungi
Earthfungi are spherical fungal growths that are native to Sotjörðr. They are dark brown in colour and are around a cubit in diameter.

## Ekraldys Toadstool
Ekraldys toadstools are slender mushrooms around 35 centimetres in height. They have grey stalks and hairy brown caps.

Mature ekraldys toadstools are poisonous and can cause nausea, fatigue, dizziness, loss of concentration, and paralysis. The poison can be removed by fermentation.

## Moonshroom
Moonshrooms are small mushrooms. They have an aquatic colour with small dark ripples, resembling the surface of a moon. They can grow up to 10 centimetres.

# Berries
## Amethyst Gorbry
Amethyst gorbry bushes are rare berry bushes found in Myrkjörðr and Veldalfheimr. Their flowers are a mix of blue and pink, and their grape-sized fruits are clear and purple. At night, they emit a very faint glow.

## Practal Gorbry
Practal gorbry bushes are common berry bushes found in Myrkjörðr and Veldalfheimr. Their flowers are muted red, and their grape-sized fruits are colored red and yellow (like an apple).
