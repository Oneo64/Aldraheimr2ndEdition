Ivycraft are servants made of vines, controlled by druidic magic.

## Description
Ivycraft appear as gangly humanoid creatures made of lush green vines. They typically have meter-long arms and legs, no torso, and no head.

Active ivycraft are bipedal, while inert ivycraft lie limp on the ground.

## Abilities
Ivycraft are fragile and do not have much strength, unable to wear armour or use heavy weapons. Their movement abilities heavily depend on the person controlling them; unskilled druids' ivycraft are clumsy, while skilled druids' ivycraft are dexterous. They are also extremely vulnerable to fire and must be kept watered, or they will die.
