A frost dragon is a great beast that dwells in cold regions of the world. Their diet consists of goats and yaks.

## Description
Frost dragons have stout sky-blue scales, blue eyes with black sclera, and a spiky tail. Their necks and bellies are covered with thick white fur. They can grow up to 27 meters long. They take 40 years to mature and live up to 300 years. Their eggs are white with a faint colourful glimmer (like opal) and are 0.5 meters high.

## Abilities
Adult frost dragons can breathe frost and ice-cold wind up to 35 meters away, for a couple of seconds, which can freeze water and encase victims in a thin layer of ice. They can use their claws to climb.

Not only that, adult frost dragons have hard scales covering their bodies, repelling most arrows and bolts, although not completely immune to them. Their strong stomachs allow them to easily withstand poison, and their thick fur allows them to survive freezing temperatures.

## Behaviour
Frost dragons are shy creatures, preferring to stay far away from civilisation where their homes and families are. They are docile as long as you don't attack them or trespass and mess with their land.
