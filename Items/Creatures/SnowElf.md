# Snow elf
Snow elves are a variant of elves that originate from the northern region of Myrkjörðr.

Defining features of a snow elf are droopy earlobes, pupilless eyes, and a sharper or more angular facial structure. They commonly have bright and fair features, such as blonde or white hair, vibrant eyes, and pale skin. Their voices are usually softer and deeper, giving them a velvety and soothing sound. Although snow elves mature by age 20, they can live up to 130 years while looking only two-thirds of their age.

Snow elves have subtle innate magic, which allows them to see more clearly in the dark, withstand brighter lights, and resist colder temperatures.

Snow elves dress themselves in woollen clothing. They are more likely to go for a longer, calf-length tunic with slits as their daily wear.
