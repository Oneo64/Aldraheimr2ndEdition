# Southern Human
Southern humans live in the southern region of Myrkjörðr. They are based on ancient Persians.

## Typical appearance
Southern humans have warmer and slightly more severe features, such as black or brown hair, brown eyes, and olive skin. Many wear linen garments, while wealthier people may wear silk.

Men usually wear lightly dyed open-front tunics secured with simple strings, simple belts, trousers, and shoes. Women usually wear flowing floor-length dresses, simple belts, and shoes, often with slightly larger sleeves.
