# Orc
Orcs are brutish humanoids who come from Neshozia. They are extremely rare to see on mainland Myrkjörðr except for the southernmost coast of the Taribian desert. They are based on prehistoric humans.

## Typical appearance
Orcs have a darker and more yellowish or greenish appearance, and typically have a more elongated or severe appearance in their facial structure. They rarely have a hair colour other than black. They tend to have more muscle and less fat.

Orcs tend to wear animal skins woven into tunics and cloaks. Wealthier groups may wear imported clothing from Taribia or other nearby mainland civilisations.
