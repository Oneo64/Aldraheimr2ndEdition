A redcap is a small, murderous, goblin-like creature that dwells in caves. When someone takes refuge in a redcap cave, it will come out and attack them.

## Description
Redcaps are small and stout, with grisly hair, large red eyes, long teeth, and red mushroom caps on their head. They dress in brown or red rags, sometimes a cloak, and wear thick fur boots.

## Abilities
Despite their small stature, redcaps are very strong. They are excellent throwers and utilise large rocks as their main weapon, throwing them at their victims.

Once they kill a victim, they will drink their blood (it goes into the mushroom cap) and soak their clothes in it, giving them a red colour.
