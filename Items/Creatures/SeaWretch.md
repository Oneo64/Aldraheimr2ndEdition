A sea wretch is a small, goblin-like creature that dwells in the water of a beach.

## Description
Sea wretches are small, goblin-like creatures with pale blue skin, eyes that glow in the dark, long claws, and webbed hands and feet. They wear scraps weathered by saltwater, but the long hairs that cover their bodies make up for the torn clothing.

## Abilities
Sea wretches come out of the ocean to roam the road whenever the sun isn't there, such as in fog, rainstorms, and nighttime. They capture humans and elves to enslave them or hold them hostage while demanding a ransom (often by sending cryptic messages). Some practice black magic, especially Gu magic.

Sea wretches are nocturnal creatures since the sun burns their skin and causes them extreme pain. They are also afraid of fire, seeing it as a threat to their vulnerability.
