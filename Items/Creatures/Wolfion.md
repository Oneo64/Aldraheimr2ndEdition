A wolfion is a cannibalistic wolf demon that dwells in mountains and heavily forested areas. They typically come out during the night to hunt, feeding on deer, elk, moose, and sometimes humans and elves.

## Description
Wolfions are tall humanoids standing at 2 and a half metres, with triangular ears, cunning yellow eyes that glow in the dark, and a long wolf-like snout. Their bodies are covered with rough, dirty brown fur, resembling dire wolves.

## Abilities
Wolfions are much stronger than humans and have claws on their hands, allowing them to deliver devastating blows. They are very agile, being able to run faster and jump farther. They can also see in the dark.

The sound of a wolfion's howl is loud and shrill, unlike a normal wolf's. When they do this, they are controlling the minds of nearby wolves. This sound also causes domesticated dogs to cower and hide.

However, wolfions do not know how to use weapons, and armour irritates their fur. They are afraid of fire, and silver burns their skin.
