A fire spirit is a natural manifestation of Talmundur originating in southern regions. They are emotionless and cold. They try to avoid forests, jungles, and other wooded areas, residing in drier places like deserts and rocky mountains.

## Description
Fire spirits are living blazes in the shape of legless humanoids. Their only facial feature is a pair of demonic eyes, resembling wavering spots of soot with bright specks inside.

## Abilities
Fire spirits can fly just as fast as someone can run. They can speak Wòrdimunag (Primordial), despite not having a mouth.

Because of their fiery body, fire spirits are able to squeeze into small spaces such as under a door, through window frames, and between cage bars. They can ignite flammable surfaces just by touching them, but they can also render their own flames harmless.

By striking the heart of a fire spirit, they are damaged much more than simply attacking its flame. Extinguishing a fire spirit's flame kills them instantly.
