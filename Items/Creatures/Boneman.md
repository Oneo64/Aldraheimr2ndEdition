A boneman is a skeleton reanimated by black magic.

## Description
Bonemen are reanimated skeletons of humanoids, sometimes equipped with weapons, shields, and armour.

Some bonemen are awakened, walking around or guarding something, while others are on the ground as a pile of bones.

## Abilities
Bonemen are dim-witted, knowing just enough to use their weapon. They cannot be trained to use magic, since they're just reanimated skeletons. They can understand the languages of their creator, but cannot speak or comprehend complex thoughts.

When a boneman is inert (bones scattered on the ground) and something triggers its awakening, its bones quickly fly together and snap into place. Then, it will equip itself with its weapons unless it cannot find them
