A great leafbug is a large insectoid creature that dwells in central Myrkjörðr. They feed on ekraldys toadstools.

## Description
Great leafbugs are enormous, green stickbugs with 12 legs. They are about a meter and a half long.

## Abilities
Great leafbugs can climb onto walls, often nestling into trees whenever they want to hide. They have bodily fluids that act as a brine to ferment their main diet (ekraldys toadstools), which is crucial since ekraldys toadstools are poisonous.
