A fire dragon is a fierce, territorial beast that dwells in hot regions of the world. Their diet consists of goats and cattle.

## Description
Fire dragons have a slim body with stony scales, dark red eyes with golden sclera, and a spiky tail. They can grow up to 30 meters long. They take 30 years to mature and live up to 250 years. Their eggs have a slightly metallic gold-bronze colour and are 0.5 meters high.

## Abilities
Adult fire dragons can breathe continuous fire up to 30 meters away and can hold it for around 4 seconds at a time. Their claws burn like fire, igniting flammable objects, and they can use them to climb.

Not only that, adult fire dragons have hard scales covering their bodies, repelling most arrows and bolts, although not completely immune to them. They resist poison with a strong stomach, and they cannot be burnt or set aflame by fire.

## Behaviour
Fire dragons are anti-social creatures, becoming territorial when their land is trespassed. Luckily, they don't seek to conquer anything in the world and would rather live near their homes.
