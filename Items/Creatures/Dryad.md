A dryad is a female fey creature native to Veldalfheimr, bound to her own tree. However, if that tree is killed, she goes into a frenzy and will attempt to kill the person who harmed their tree.

## Description
Dryads have the appearance of ethereally beautiful women who have shining skin, fair hair, and a faint light green aura. Most dryads wear bright, flowing white dresses.

Dryads mature by 20 and live up to 300 years old.

## Abilities
Dryads can carve all types of magical staves (without limits) and perform all druidry. She can also control dreams, possess creatures, and willingly travel from Veldalfheimr to Myrkjörðr and vice versa. She always knows the location of her tree, unless it is destroyed.

Dryads can speak both Druidic and Sylvan.
