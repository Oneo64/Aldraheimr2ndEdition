# Moon elf
Moon elves are a variant of elves that originate from the central Myrkjörðr, especially from the Mountains of Ranûdel.

Defining features of a moon elf are long sharp ears, pupilless eyes, and a softer or rounder facial structure. They commonly have ethereal and flowing features, such as straight dark purple hair, blue or grey eyes, and pale blue skin. Some elves, especially those who live in the valleys, have a darker complexion with black hair and dark blue skin. Their voices are usually softer and deeper, giving them a velvety and soothing sound.

Although moon elves mature by age 30, they can live up to 200 years while looking only half their age.

Moon elves typically wear cotton garments. The men usually wear a tunic, a simple belt, trousers, and boots. The women usually wear flowing dresses with larger sleeves, a simple belt (sometimes), and shoes or sandals. A moon elf's clothing will almost certainly be dyed in a natural colour, such as brown, green, or teal.

Moon elves have subtle innate magic, which allows them to see more clearly in the dark, be lighter than usual, and have a keen sense of balance. They also act differently in some ways, being calmer and gentler normally, and more shy around non-elves.
