# Northern Human
Northern humans live in the northern region of Myrkjörðr and make up the population of the kingdoms of Heorthland, Oddarsveldi, Rotherland, and Þeyrík. They are based on the Germanic peoples.

## Typical appearance
Northern humans have fair and pale features, and typically have wavy hair and angular noses. They tend to keep their hair longer than others; for example, men's hair is typically shoulder-length, while women's hair can range from shoulder-length to waist-length. Men are more likely to grow a longer beard.

Northern humans wear woollen clothing sewn using simple rectangular cuts, using crude methods to keep it from falling off their shoulders. Unlike other races, most northern humans carry some sort of weapon on them.

An ordinary man might wear a knee-length tunic, a simple belt, trousers, and shoes or boots, while an ordinary woman might wear a tunic dress, a simple belt, and shoes. Many may wear a large, simple, rectangular blanket as a cloak. Wealthier groups may wear a linen undergarment and use metal for higher-quality clothing.
