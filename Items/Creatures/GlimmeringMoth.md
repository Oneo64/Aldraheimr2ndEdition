A glimmering moth is a large insectoid creature that dwells in the Mountains of Ranûdel. They feed on moonshrooms.

## Description
Glimmering moths are moth-like creatures 5 times larger than a brown house moth, probably around the size of your hand. They are brown with small blue streaks.
