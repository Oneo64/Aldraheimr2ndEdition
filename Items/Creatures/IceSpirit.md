An ice spirit is a natural manifestation of Talmundur originating in northern regions. They are social "creatures", usually forming groups of up to ten members. They try to stick to the northernmost areas so that they don't melt.

## Description
Ice spirits are man-shaped ice humanoids. They don't have a face, but they can see normally.

## Abilities
Ice spirits can speak Wòrdimunag (Primordial) despite not having a mouth. They can pick up weapons, wear armour, throw things, and do anything else a human can do.

When their bodies are damaged, ice spirits possess a special ability to fix themselves by melding cold, icy water onto themselves and letting it freeze, filling out their wounds.

However, ice spirits are vulnerable to fire, temperate weather, and water. Coming into contact with any of these starts to degrade their bodies.
