A crawler is a supernatural monster that dwells in Neshozian forests.

## Description
The body of a crawler is a bunched mess of thick roots and lianas, with several "legs" protruding from the core. When stretched out, they can be as long as three meters.

## Abilities
Crawlers are strong, durable, and stealthy, and can grapple their enemies to strangle them or drag them away. Luckily, they don't know how to use weapons. Since they are vulnerable to fire, they fear and avoid it.
