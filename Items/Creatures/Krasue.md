A krasue is a cursed woman's spirit that dwells in forests and jungles, especially the southern ones. One may become a krasue if she has sinned and was cursed to become one.

## Description
Krasues are the head of a young woman, with her internal organs hanging from the bottom of her neck. Sometimes they may have a faint glow around them like a will-o'-the-wisp.

## Abilities
Krasues move by floating in the air. They feed on blood, particularly human blood. They come out to hunt during the night and hide away once day comes.
