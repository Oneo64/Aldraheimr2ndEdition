A wisp is an uncursed free spirit that, under rare circumstances, did not pass into full death. One may become a wisp if they die whilst suffering from arcane poison, naturally pass away in their sleep, or via sorcerous rituals.

## Description
Wisps look like a tiny orb of light emanating faint mist. The light from their orbs only provides enough to faintly illuminate a small area.

## Abilities
Wisps can fly in any direction they like, go through objects, and speak to the living by entering their mind. They can speak the languages they knew before death.

If a wisp doesn't enter the mind of a living being before speaking to them, it is basically mute. This also means that they cannot communicate with other spirits and undead, since they aren't living beings.
