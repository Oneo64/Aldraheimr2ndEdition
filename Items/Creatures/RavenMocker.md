A raven mocker is an evil spirit who happens to be a witch.

## Description
Raven mockers are human-like figures cloaked in black, with black wings attached from their arms to their sides. Their faces are withered and aged, resembling an old hag.

## Abilities
Raven mockers prey on sick humans during the night, sometimes posing as a doctor or nurse to get closer to them. Once their victim is left unguarded, they will approach them and eat their heart to increase their lifespan.

Raven mockers can use their wings to fly in the air. If a raven mocker dives towards something, it lets out an uncanny screech. Many people say that if one hears this screech, it's an omen of death.
