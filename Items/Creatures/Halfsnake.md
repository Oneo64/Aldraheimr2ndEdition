A half-snake is a hybrid between a human and a snake. They are typically greedy and would kill for gold.

## Description
Half-snakes have dark hair, yellow slit eyes, pale flawless skin, and scales on their shoulders and hips. They are slightly taller than humans, being about two inches taller on average. They wear flowing tunics that resemble Greek clothing.

Since half-snakes are part snake, they bleed thin, pale blood.

## Abilities
Half-snakes have sharp nails as claws and can extend them like a cat. They have tough skin (allowing them to go barefoot) and are resistant to some poisons, although certain flowers are still poisonous to them. They also possess a special power that allows them to detect fake gold by touching it.

Female half-snakes usually become temptresses, possessing the ability to charm people by looking directly into their eyes.
