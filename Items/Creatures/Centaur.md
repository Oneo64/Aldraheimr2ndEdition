A centaur is a half-man, half-horse found in both Myrkjörðr and Veldalfheimr. They live in groups, small kingdoms, tribes, etc. The most well-known centaur kingdoms in Myrkjörðr are Heirfgria and Vatakond.

## Description
Centaurs are a hybrid race of man and horse, with the upper body of a human and the lower body of a horse. Their horse hair extends all the way up to their neck and wrists. They mature by 20 and can live up to 150 years old.

## Abilities
Centaurs can gallop, canter, trot, and do horse things just like a normal horse (except neighing). Those who originated from Veldalfheimr speak Druidic and Sylvan, while those who originated from Myrkjörðr might speak either Druidic, Sylvan, Sylvendric, any elvish variant, or a combination of the four.
