A troll is a northern forest creature that lives in tribes.

## Description
Trolls are slightly shorter than a man, bearing dark hair, black beady eyes that glow in the dark (like a cat), rough grey skin like stone, and large flat noses. They wear mostly browns and greys.

## Abilities
Trolls are much stronger than men, but not as strong as draugar. They are a lot dumber than humans, rarely born with equal intelligence. Upon stepping into sunlight, they will slowly turn into stone and die if they remain too long, which is why they dwell in dark forests. They can see in the dark.

## Behaviour
Trolls are half-social creatures, living in small tribes. They are extremely shy with outsiders, sometimes even attacking if they feel threatened. When a troll is born and proven the wisest of their community, they are chosen to become the chieftain when their current one passes away.

Trolls have a strong dislike for metal, calling it unnatural and wicked. However, even when other humanoid races have continuously proven their "wickedness" by forging metal items, they are still not openly hostile when encountering one of the races.
