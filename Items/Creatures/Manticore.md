A manticore is a monstrous being that lives in a jungle or with a daeva tribe. Their diet is human and elven meat.

## Description
A manticores has the head of a man, the body of a lion, and the tail of a scorpion. Its eyes are vibrant blue like a sapphire, its hair is the colour of blood, and its mouth bears three rows of sharp teeth. Its tail has a stinger similar to a porcupine quill.

## Abilities
Manticores usually swallow their victim whole, digesting them inside their stomach. They can fire the stinger on their tail, which hurts like a metal arrow and causes numbness to spread over the bodies of anyone who gets shot. The tail's stinger regrows in about a day. They cannot be burnt or set aflame by ordinary fire.
