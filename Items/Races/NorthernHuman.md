# Northern Human
Northern humans are humans who live in the northern region of Myrkjörðr. They typically have fair and pale features, especially wavy hair and angular noses. They tend to keep their hair longer than others; for example, men's hair is typically shoulder-length, while women's hair can range from shoulder-length to waist-length. Men are more likely to grow a longer beard.

Northern humans wear woollen clothing sewn using simple rectangular cuts, using crude methods to keep it from falling off their shoulders. Unlike other races, most northern humans carry some sort of weapon on them.
